Kipi & Nooli App

A personal publishing tool for the Kipi & Nooli children's YouTube channel.

What it does

Kipi & Nooli App is a private, single-user tool. Its only job is to upload finished Kipi & Nooli videos, with their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, to the owner's own YouTube channel and to schedule when they are published.

It uses the YouTube Data API (YouTube API Services) and signs in with the channel owner's Google account through OAuth. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not access anyone else's account or data.
